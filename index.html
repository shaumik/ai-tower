HOW TO PLAY

Drag to pan the battlefield. Tap to select.

¤ Minerals come from mining, not kills. Buy miners at the core; they haul crystal loads to a drop-off. More miners = more income — and raiders hunt them.

⚡ Power limits your turrets. Generators add power. Depots add remote drop-offs and +2 miner cap. Walls block the ground routes — shape the approach, but they can be broken.

Crystal fields run dry. Expanding toward far fields pays more and defends worse.

⚠ RECALL pulls miners to safety at the core — no income while they hide. Waves launch on a countdown; calling one early pays a bonus.

Between waves, directives offer one-wave trades and ⚙ CORE OS sells permanent upgrades. Unspent minerals earn interest every wave.
