HOW TO PLAY

Drag to orbit and climb the spire. Tap a socket to build.

Threats climb the ramp from the base. If they reach the summit, the core takes damage. Lose all 10 integrity and the spire falls.

¤ Salvage comes from kills — and unspent salvage earns interest every wave, so hoarding pays… until it doesn't.

⚡ Power limits how much you can run. Generators add power but occupy defense sockets.

Repulsors throw climbers off the spire. The higher the fall, the bigger the salvage bonus.

Fliers ignore the ramp and rise straight up — cover your airspace.
